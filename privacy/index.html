Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Asiyen Enterprise (Pty) Ltd ("Asiyen", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. It covers the Asiyen mobile application and this website.

Information We Collect

When you open an account

  • Your mobile number — used to create your account and to verify you by one-time password (OTP)
  • Your name and date of birth
  • The display name you choose. Other Asiyen users see this name, followed by the first letter of your surname (for example “Bra T · M.”), instead of your full name.
  • Device information and the internet (IP) address you connect from — used for security and fraud prevention, and shown to you in your account's security activity

When you verify your identity

Verification is required before you can top up past R1 000 or receive transfers past R1 500, deposit more than R2 000 in any 30-day period, or withdraw more than R1 000 at once. To verify you, we collect:

  • Your identity document type and number (South African ID or passport)
  • A photograph of your identity document
  • A photograph of yourself, to confirm the document belongs to you

Once you are verified, we send your identity number to our payment provider, Ozow, each time you top up your wallet. Ozow uses it to confirm that the bank account paying in belongs to you. If you are not verified, we do not send it.

When you are paid out (Merchant and Agent accounts only)

  • Your bank, your branch code, and your bank account number

Customer accounts cannot withdraw to a bank account, and we do not collect banking details from them, except to return your balance when you close your account (see below). How we handle these details is set out separately below.

Withdrawing money as a Customer

Withdrawals to a bank account are available to Merchant and Agent accounts only. A Customer account can spend its wallet balance on payments, transfers, vouchers, airtime and data, but cannot pay it out to a bank.

This does not mean your money is locked in. If you decide to close your Customer account while there is still money in your wallet, contact us at admin@asiyen.com (or use Delete my account in the app) and we will return the remaining balance to you, with no fee. We will pay it into a bank account in your own name within 14 days after we have received your request, completed identity verification, and confirmed your bank details. Your wallet balance belongs to you.

As you use Asiyen

  • Your transaction history — payments, top-ups, withdrawals, transfers, voucher redemptions and airtime purchases
  • The mobile number you send airtime or data to, which may belong to someone else
  • Reports you make to us, including payment disputes and complaints about an agent

If you order stickers by post

  • Your shipping address
  • The proof of payment you upload

Why We Collect It

  • To create and operate your wallet, and to carry out the transactions you instruct — we need this information to provide the service you have asked for
  • To verify your identity, and to meet our obligations under South African financial and anti-money-laundering law
  • To pay withdrawals into your bank account
  • To detect, investigate and prevent fraud and misuse
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Your Banking Details

We treat banking details as narrowly as we can while still being able to pay you.

  • We store your full bank account number only in encrypted form. The encryption key is held separately from our database, so the stored number cannot be read from the database alone. It is decrypted only at the moment a payout is sent to our payment provider.
  • Your full account number is encrypted before it leaves our systems and is passed to our payment provider solely to make the payment. Each payout uses its own encryption key.
  • Your banking details cannot be changed directly from the app in the way ordinary profile information can. Changes go through a controlled process, so that someone who gains access to your device cannot quietly redirect your money to another account.
  • You may withdraw only to a bank account held in your own name.

Your Identity Documents

Photographs of your identity document and of yourself are held in private storage. They are not published, not shared for any purpose other than verifying you, and not accessible from a public link.

When a member of our staff reviews your verification, the system creates a temporary link that expires after a few minutes, and records who accessed your documents and when. That record cannot be edited or deleted.

Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where it is necessary to provide the service:

  • Ozow — our payment provider, to process deposits into your wallet and to pay withdrawals into your bank account. If you are verified, this includes your identity number when you top up, as described above.
  • SIMcloud — to deliver airtime and data to the mobile number you specify
  • Bird (formerly MessageBird) — to send your one-time passwords and security alerts by SMS, such as when your bank details or account settings change
  • Resend — to send security alerts by email to Asiyen staff. These can include the name of an agent or user involved in an action we are monitoring.
  • Supabase — our hosting provider, which stores our data on our behalf
  • Sentry — to receive crash and error reports from the app so we can fix problems. A report contains technical details about the error and your device (model, Android version and app version), never your name, phone number, ID number, balance or payment details. Reports are stored in the European Union.
  • Cloudflare — to run the security check when you ask for a sign-in code, which helps stop automated abuse. For that check Cloudflare sees your internet (IP) address and basic details of your device.
  • Paxi — the courier, where you have ordered stickers by post

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, including to a court, a regulator, or a law-enforcement authority acting under lawful authority.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Our data is hosted on Supabase servers located in the European Union (Ireland). This means your personal information is transferred outside South Africa. The European Union has data-protection laws that provide a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Encrypted backups of our database are kept with Cloudflare in the European Union. Only Asiyen holds the key that opens them.

How Long We Keep It

  • Transaction records are kept for at least five years, as South African financial law requires.
  • Identity verification records are kept for at least five years after your account closes, for the same reason.
  • Banking details are kept while your account is open and you may still be paid out. You can ask us to remove them at any time; doing so means we cannot pay you until you provide them again.
  • Other account information is kept while your account is active.

Where the law requires us to keep a record, we cannot delete it on request — but we will tell you when that is the case and why.

Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you, and receive a copy of it
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • Ask us to delete your account and your information, subject to the retention periods above (see how to delete your account)
  • Object to how we are processing your information
  • Complain to us, and to the Information Regulator

To exercise any of these rights, email admin@asiyen.com. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can, and in any event within the period the law allows.

Keeping Your Account Secure

Your PIN is never stored in a readable form and is never checked on your phone — it is verified on our servers, so that a modified app cannot bypass it. You are responsible for keeping your PIN and your device secure. Tell us immediately if you believe someone has accessed your account.

You can choose to make small payments without entering your PIN, up to limits you set. It is off unless you turn it on. Turning it on or raising the limits needs your PIN, and we send you an SMS when you do. Sending money, withdrawals, airtime and changes to your bank details always need your PIN.

If Something Goes Wrong

If personal information under our control is accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Children

Asiyen is not for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that we hold information about a child, we will delete it.

Cookies

The Asiyen mobile app does not use cookies. This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies.
