Delete your Asiyen account

Last updated: October 2026

You can ask us to delete your Asiyen account at any time. We review every request ourselves, so nothing is deleted straight away.

How to ask

  • In the app: open Settings and tap Delete my account (in the latest version of the app).
  • By email: write to admin@asiyen.com with the subject “Delete my account” and the mobile number registered to your account.

We then contact you on your registered number to confirm that the request came from you, before we act on it.

Money in your wallet

  • Any money left in your wallet belongs to you. We return it, in full and with no fee, to a bank account in your own name within 14 days after we have received your request, completed identity verification, and confirmed your bank details.
  • Merchants and Agents get their full balance back on closure, even under R100, with no fee.
  • Agents must first hand over any cash they hold for Asiyen.

What we remove

Everything we are not required to keep, including your display name, your mobile number (so the account can no longer be used), your banking details once your balance has been paid, and your PIN.

What the law requires us to keep

  • Transaction records, for at least five years.
  • Identity verification records (your ID number, document photos and selfie), for at least five years after your account closes.
  • A record of your request and how we handled it.

We keep these securely and use them only to meet our legal obligations. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.
