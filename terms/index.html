1. Who may use Asiyen

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must register using a South African mobile number registered in your own name (RICA).
  • You may hold only one account. This does not apply to accounts that Asiyen runs for its own operations. Accounts are personal and may not be transferred.
  • You must provide accurate information and keep it up to date.

2. Account roles

Asiyen accounts are one of three types:

  • Customer — loads funds and pays fares, sends money, and buys airtime or data.
  • Merchant — receives fare payments through an assigned QR sticker and may withdraw earnings to a bank account.
  • Agent — sells stickers and vouchers, activates merchants, and earns commission under a separate agent agreement.

3. Verification and limits

Accounts begin with entry-level limits. You must complete identity verification before you can top up or redeem a voucher that would take your balance above R1 000, receive a transfer from another Asiyen user that would take your balance above R1 500, deposit more than R2 000 in any 30-day period, or withdraw more than R1 000 at once. Fare payments you receive are not limited in this way. You verify by submitting a valid South African ID or passport.

We may decline or reverse a verification, request further information, or apply lower limits where we are required to do so or where we reasonably suspect fraud or misuse. Verification decisions are reviewed by a person, and you may contact us to query an outcome.

4. Your wallet balance

Your wallet balance is money you have loaded, and it belongs to you. We record every rand of it against your account, and we do not use it for our operating expenses.

When you top up, your payment is collected by our payment processor, Ozow, and held in a balance maintained for Asiyen, from which withdrawals to merchants and agents are paid. We may also add our own money to that balance so that withdrawals can be paid promptly. Funds not needed for withdrawals are settled to a bank account in Asiyen's name that is used only for customer funds and is kept separate from the account we use to run the business. The fees we earn are moved to our operating account only once they have been earned.

Your wallet balance is not a bank deposit, is not a savings or investment product, does not earn interest, and is not covered by deposit insurance.

You are responsible for keeping your PIN and device secure. Notify us immediately if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

5. Fees

Fees are displayed in the app before you confirm any transaction. At present:

  • Your first wallet top-up is free. Thereafter a top-up fee applies, shown before you confirm.
  • The minimum top-up is R50.
  • A flat fee applies to each withdrawal.
  • A fee is deducted when an Asiyen voucher is redeemed.
  • Sending money to another Asiyen user costs R3, paid by the sender on top of the amount sent, when either of you has a Customer account. Transfers between Merchant and Agent accounts carry a fee of between R3 and R12, shown before you confirm.

We may change our fees. Where we do, the current fee is always the one displayed in the app at the time you confirm a transaction.

6. Paying a fare and sending money

Scanning a merchant's QR sticker and confirming an amount authorises an immediate transfer from your wallet to that merchant. You are responsible for entering the correct amount. Because payment is immediate, a completed fare payment cannot be reversed by us unilaterally.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact the merchant in the first instance and then email us at admin@asiyen.com.

Sending money to another Asiyen user is also immediate. Before you confirm, the app shows the name of the person you are sending to: check that it is the person you mean. If you send money to the wrong person, we cannot reverse the transfer ourselves. Contact us at admin@asiyen.com as soon as possible and we will ask the recipient to return it, but we cannot guarantee that they will. The transfer fee is not refunded.

7. Withdrawals

Withdrawals to a bank account are available to Merchant and Agent accounts only. Customer accounts cannot withdraw. You must withdraw to a bank account held in your own name. We may delay or decline a withdrawal where verification is incomplete or where we are required to investigate.

Withdrawals are reviewed and sent as real-time payments, normally within an hour between 07:00 and 19:00, any day of the week. A withdrawal requested outside those hours is sent from 07:00. Your own bank may occasionally take longer to show it. Confirmation that a payout has completed can reach us up to 24 hours after your bank has already received the money, so a withdrawal still showing as pending in the app does not mean it has failed. If a payout cannot be completed, the amount and the withdrawal fee are returned to your wallet.

If you close your account while money remains in your wallet, we return the whole balance to you, with no fee. This applies to Customer, Merchant and Agent accounts, and to a Merchant or Agent balance below the R100 withdrawal minimum. We pay it into a bank account in your own name within 14 days after we have received your request, completed identity verification, and confirmed your bank details. An Agent must first hand over any cash they hold for Asiyen. See how to close your account.

8. Vouchers

Asiyen vouchers are sold in cash by authorised agents and are redeemed in the app. A voucher is valid for three years from the date it is issued. Vouchers are not refundable for cash and may only be redeemed once.

If you are asked to pay more than a voucher's face value, report it to us at admin@asiyen.com. We investigate such reports and may suspend the agent concerned.

9. Airtime and data

Airtime and data are supplied by third-party providers. Once a purchase is successfully delivered to the mobile number you specify, it cannot be reversed. You are responsible for entering the correct number.

10. Stickers

QR stickers remain identified to the merchant they are activated for. Stickers may not be transferred, copied, or reproduced. We may deactivate a sticker that is reported lost, damaged, misused, or associated with fraud; a deactivated sticker can no longer receive payments.

Delivery Policy

Asiyen supplies both digital services and a physical item, and delivery differs between them.

  • Wallet top-ups are credited to your wallet as soon as payment is confirmed by our payment provider, normally within minutes. Where a payment is confirmed but your wallet is not credited, contact us and we will resolve it.
  • Fare payments and transfers between Asiyen users are delivered immediately on confirmation.
  • Airtime and data are delivered directly to the mobile number specified, normally within minutes of purchase.
  • Voucher redemptions credit your wallet immediately on successful redemption.
  • QR stickers can be handed over in person by an authorised Asiyen agent at the point of sale, or couriered directly to you if ordered online. A sticker becomes usable once it has been activated against your Merchant account — this can happen either through an agent in person, or remotely once a courier order is confirmed received. An agent is not required to activate a sticker.
  • Withdrawals are paid to your nominated bank account. Withdrawals are reviewed and sent as real-time payments, normally within an hour between 07:00 and 19:00, any day of the week. A withdrawal requested outside those hours is sent from 07:00. If a payout cannot be completed, the amount and the withdrawal fee are returned to your wallet.

There are no delivery charges for digital services. The price of a sticker is the price quoted by the agent at the point of sale.
