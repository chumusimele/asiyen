Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Asiyen Enterprise (Pty) Ltd ("Asiyen", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Information We Collect

  • Phone number — used for authentication via OTP
  • Full name — optional, used for personalisation
  • Transaction history — payments, top-ups, withdrawals, transfers
  • Device information — for security and fraud prevention

How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity via one-time password (OTP)
  • To process payments, transfers and withdrawals
  • To maintain your wallet balance and transaction records
  • To provide customer support
  • To detect and prevent fraud

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers located in the European Union (Ireland) with industry-standard encryption. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share data only with:

  • Payment processors — to process deposits, withdrawals and payouts
  • SMS providers (MessageBird) — to deliver OTP verification codes

Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by Asiyen
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to processing of your personal information

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as required by law. Transaction records are retained for a minimum of 5 years in accordance with financial regulations.

Cookies

The Asiyen mobile app does not use cookies. This website uses minimal analytics to understand visitor behaviour — no personally identifiable information is collected.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 2026

These terms govern your use of the Asiyen mobile application and related services operated by Asiyen Enterprise (Pty) Ltd ("Asiyen", "we", "us"), a company registered in the Republic of South Africa. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

1. Who may use Asiyen

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must register using a South African mobile number registered in your own name (RICA).
  • You may hold only one account. Accounts are personal and may not be transferred.
  • You must provide accurate information and keep it up to date.

2. Account roles

Asiyen accounts are one of three types:

  • Customer — loads funds and pays fares, sends money, and buys airtime or data.
  • Merchant — receives fare payments through an assigned QR sticker and may withdraw earnings to a bank account.
  • Agent — sells stickers and vouchers, activates merchants, and earns commission under a separate agent agreement.

3. Verification and limits

Accounts begin with entry-level limits. To hold a balance above R1 000, deposit more than R2 000 in any 30-day period, or withdraw more than R1 000 at once, you must complete identity verification by submitting a valid South African ID or passport.

We may decline or reverse a verification, request further information, or apply lower limits where we are required to do so or where we reasonably suspect fraud or misuse. Verification decisions are reviewed by a person, and you may contact us to query an outcome.

4. Your wallet balance

Funds in your Asiyen wallet are held in a separate account maintained for the benefit of users and are not used for our operating expenses. Your wallet balance is not a bank deposit, is not a savings or investment product, and does not earn interest.

You are responsible for keeping your PIN and device secure. Notify us immediately if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

5. Fees

Fees are displayed in the app before you confirm any transaction. At present:

  • Your first wallet top-up is free. Thereafter a top-up fee applies, shown before you confirm.
  • The minimum top-up is R50.
  • A flat fee applies to each withdrawal.
  • A fee is deducted when an Asiyen voucher is redeemed.
  • Transfers between Asiyen users may carry a fee depending on the account types involved.

We may change our fees. Where we do, the current fee is always the one displayed in the app at the time you confirm a transaction.

6. Paying a fare

Scanning a merchant's QR sticker and confirming an amount authorises an immediate transfer from your wallet to that merchant. You are responsible for entering the correct amount. Because payment is immediate, a completed fare payment cannot be reversed by us unilaterally.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact the merchant in the first instance and then raise it with us through the app.

7. Withdrawals

Withdrawals to a bank account are available to Merchant and Agent accounts only. Customer accounts cannot withdraw. You must withdraw to a bank account held in your own name. We may delay or decline a withdrawal where verification is incomplete or where we are required to investigate.

8. Vouchers

Asiyen vouchers are sold in cash by authorised agents and are redeemed in the app. A voucher expires 90 days after it is issued if it has not been redeemed, after which it has no value. Vouchers are not refundable for cash and may only be redeemed once.

If you are asked to pay more than a voucher's face value, report it in the app. We investigate such reports and may suspend the agent concerned.

9. Airtime and data

Airtime and data are supplied by third-party providers. Once a purchase is successfully delivered to the mobile number you specify, it cannot be reversed. You are responsible for entering the correct number.

10. Stickers

QR stickers remain identified to the merchant they are activated for. Stickers may not be transferred, copied, or reproduced. We may deactivate a sticker that is reported lost, damaged, misused, or associated with fraud; a deactivated sticker can no longer receive payments.

11. Acceptable use

You may not use Asiyen to launder money, finance illegal activity, evade tax, impersonate another person, or process transactions on behalf of an undisclosed third party. You may not attempt to interfere with, reverse-engineer, or gain unauthorised access to the service.

You may close your account at any time by contacting us, after withdrawing or spending your remaining balance.

13. Availability

We aim to keep Asiyen available at all times but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. Deposits, withdrawals, and airtime purchases depend on banks, payment providers, and network operators outside our control.

Delivery Policy

Asiyen supplies both digital services and a physical item, and delivery differs between them.

  • Wallet top-ups are credited to your wallet as soon as payment is confirmed by our payment provider, normally within minutes. Where a payment is confirmed but your wallet is not credited, contact us and we will resolve it.
  • Fare payments and transfers between Asiyen users are delivered immediately on confirmation.
  • Airtime and data are delivered directly to the mobile number specified, normally within minutes of purchase.
  • Voucher redemptions credit your wallet immediately on successful redemption.
  • QR stickers can be handed over in person by an authorised Asiyen agent at the point of sale, or couriered directly to you if ordered online. A sticker becomes usable once it has been activated against your Merchant account — this can happen either through an agent in person, or remotely once a courier order is confirmed received. An agent is not required to activate a sticker.
  • Withdrawals are paid to your nominated bank account. Instant methods normally arrive within minutes; other methods may take longer depending on your bank.

There are no delivery charges for digital services. The price of a sticker is the price quoted by the agent at the point of sale.
