Simple by design

Three steps to cashless

Asiyen removes the friction of cash from your daily commute — no change, no hassle, no waiting.

01
📲

Download & top up

Download Asiyen, create your wallet and add money via EFT, PayShap or an Asiyen voucher from your local agent.

02
📷

Scan the sticker

When you board your taxi, open Asiyen and scan the QR sticker on the seat or window. Enter your fare amount.

03
✅

Payment confirmed

Your wallet is debited instantly. The driver receives a notification. Both parties get a transaction record.

P2P Transfers

Send money to any Asiyen user instantly via their phone number. No bank account needed — wallet to wallet in seconds.

🎟️

Voucher top-ups

No bank account? No problem. Buy an Asiyen voucher from a local agent with cash and redeem it in the app.

⚡

Fast withdrawals

Merchants withdraw earnings straight to their bank account. Withdrawals are reviewed and sent as real-time payments, normally within an hour between 07:00 and 19:00, any day of the week.

🧾

Ping confirmation

Every fare sends the merchant a ping. They tap once to confirm it, so both sides have a verified payment record.
